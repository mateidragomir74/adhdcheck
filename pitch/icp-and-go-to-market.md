# Target customers (ICP) and go-to-market

Team decision, 20 Oct 2026. **Take this into account in the pitch**: say it on slide 7 and keep appendix slide A2 ready for questions. Both are already in `pitch/ADHD_Check_Pitch.pptx`.

## Target customers (ICP)

| # | Customer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Parents | Of children aged 6 to 12; they decide whether to seek an assessment |
| 2 | ADHD associations | In each country we advertise in; they reach and advise families |
| 3 | Teachers | They notice attention problems in class |
| 4 | Nurses | Children play the game in the waiting room before the visit, so the doctor gets a first orientation and saves time |
| 5 | GPs (general practitioners) | A first orientation before referral |

## Go-to-market

1. Contact the Romanian ADHD associations and the leading doctors on ADHD in Romania (key opinion leaders).
2. Co-develop and keep improving the app with them.
3. Their validation drives adoption.
4. Develop under a quality management system per ISO 13485.
5. CE marking under EU MDR as a class IIa medical device (expected, Rule 11), for the European market, starting in Romania.

## In the pitch (one sentence)

"We reach families through ADHD associations and the doctors they trust, co-develop with them, then enter Europe with ISO 13485 and CE marking as a class IIa device."

## Be ready for this question

The intended purpose today names parents only (`docs/02`, section 2.1). Nurses, GPs and teachers as users widen it, and a teacher using it with a child needs the parent's consent. Answer: "Parents first; professional and school use come later, with their own consent and validation."
