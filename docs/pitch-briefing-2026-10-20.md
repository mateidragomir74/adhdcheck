## What was said, with the time it was said

Taken from the transcript below. Nothing added; the structure of the list is only the order of the talk.

- **Why pitch (00:29 to 01:19):** solve a problem, attract partners, convince people the idea is relevant, beat the competition.
- **Read the jury criteria (03:00 to 03:44):** the jury evaluates against written criteria. Know what the people are buying: the solution or how it is managed.
- **Choose the presenter fast (04:31 to 04:44):** the presenter focuses on the pitch, the rest of the team helps that person.
- **Rehearse (07:21, 27:06 to 27:18):** at least 5 times, ideally 10. Keep 5 to 10 seconds to find the right slide for a question: "Excellent question, I have a slide for that."
- **Template (08:12 onward):** project name and team name, then "we help target users solve the problem through solution". Then problem, solution, what makes it different, impact and validation, delivery and next steps, team. Optional support slides for Q and A.
- **Problem (08:35 to 10:25):** cross-check with scientific sources, not only an AI answer. Give a country, a target population, a health priority and a consequence. Cite the source of each number, recent and trustworthy. For Romania he named the OECD State of Health in Romania (2025 edition).
- **Solution (10:30 to 11:27):** who it serves (user, beneficiary, decision maker), one sentence, two key features, value, why it fits the workflow. "Use less words, but master them." Do not put on a slide a word you cannot explain; the trust is gone at "I don't know".
- **Show it (11:27 onward):** build something with AI, an app or an interface, "make us dream". It must connect to the problem and the solution.
- **Difference (12:44 to 14:00):** find an angle, even a small but relevant one. Regulation can be that angle (an unregulated device is not trusted by the doctor). Existing solution, what it does well, its limit, your improvement. Be honest about what exists.
- **Reality versus guideline (14:00 to 15:12):** show the gap between the ideal and what patients really do.
- **Validation (15:17 to 16:36):** a quick Google Form shared on Facebook (not Instagram, he said sick people are on Facebook) can bring about 100 answers by next morning. Then say what the answers showed.
- **Delivery (17:23 to 18:30):** key resources, partners, adoption route, risks. Is there enough money for the customer to buy? Safety and privacy if the patient is touched. Milestones and next steps (user, partner, owner, success).
- **Team (18:30 to 20:08):** show roles (business, technology, story with a scientific background, user experience), names and good individual pictures, not one group picture.
- **Business model (20:57 to 24:25):** optional. Who pays and why. Do not show a canvas you cannot explain. The healthcare impact lens he named: patients better, population better, cost down, carers' balance, health equity.
- **AI (26:49 to 27:06):** use it, but understand what it puts in front of you.
