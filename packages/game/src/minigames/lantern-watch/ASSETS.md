# Lantern Watch: assets

No new third-party asset.

- Reused: the Bridge Wait world, Luma, particles, Web Audio synth (chime, babble, river) and the Kenney CC0 1.0 sound samples self-hosted under `apps/web/public/games/bridge-wait/audio/` (listed with sources in `../bridge-wait/ASSETS.md`).
- Made in this repo: the lanterns, the firefly, the moth and the lily pad (`props.ts`, `scene.ts`), built from three.js primitives; the practice hand SVG (same drawing as Bridge Wait, `ui.ts`).
- Calibration rules: `../wake-fireflies/logic.ts` (PR #27, this repo).
- Library: three 0.186.1, MIT, already a dependency of `@adhdcheck/game`.
